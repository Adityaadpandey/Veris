Here's a more natural version. It has a narrator (you), two short lines for the journalist, and a few quick comment voices you can record with friends. It runs about 3:00 at a relaxed pace. Timings are approximate.

**How to read the cues:** _italics_ tell you how to say a line. **(beat)** is a one-second pause. **…** means let the line trail off. **Bold** words get stressed.

"AI slop." / "Check the hands, bro." / "Deepfake. Hundred percent."

**Recording tips**

- **Smile on the upbeat parts.** In sections 4, 5, 8 and 10 it changes your tone even though nobody sees it.
- **Hold the pauses.** Where it says (beat), stop fully. Those silences are where the music and effects land.
- **Record voices separately.** The journalist and comment voices work best from two or three friends, each recorded on their own so I can layer them.
- **If it runs long,** cut "Days later, his name was a joke" and "Sort of." first.

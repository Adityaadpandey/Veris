Technical

How it works, exactly.

For engineers. Three stages, six steps, one root of trust: the chip.

STAGE 1

Physical capture

Secured at the source.

1
CaptureShoot as usual. Veris takes its own image too.
2
Attest in hardwareA TPM 2.0 chip signs the hash. The key never leaves it.
STAGE 2

Cryptographic proof

Automatic and private.

3
ProveA zero-knowledge proof hides identity, GPS and serial.
4
StoreImage and proof go to IPFS and Filecoin.
5
CertifyChecked on-chain; an ERC-1155 token is minted.
STAGE 3

Public verification

Anyone, no account.

6
VerifyDrop it on veris.live/claim: VERIFIED or NO RECORD (not the same as fake).

The proof, in four lines

h = H(I)Hash the image.
σ = Sign(skD, h ‖ H(μ))The chip signs it.
π ← Prove{(pkD, σ, μ): Verify = 1 ∧ pkD ∈ R}Prove it, reveal nothing.
Accept(I′) ⇔ H(I′) = h ∧ VerifyZK(π) = 1Photo plus public record.

Dual capture

Veris signs a second image at the same instant. If both show the same scene, the main photo inherits the proof.
